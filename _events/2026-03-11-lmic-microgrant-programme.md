  GLC launched a two-track micro-grant programme supporting data
  collection and harmonisation activities for the Global Light
  Commons: Track 1 (EUR 3,000) for data collection and harmonisation
  in LMICs, and Track 2 (EUR 1,500) for harmonising existing datasets
  to GLC standards.

  Applications closed 30 April 2026, reviewed on a rolling basis. The
  programme was designed to lower barriers to participation and
  accelerate a globally inclusive, interoperable resource for light
  exposure and visual experience research.
