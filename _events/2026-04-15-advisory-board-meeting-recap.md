  GLC held its inaugural Advisory Board meeting on 15 April 2026, bringing
  together all seven board members to review progress, share results from
  the MeLiDos campaign, and gather strategic input on data harmonisation
  and global representation.
description_2: >-
  The MeLiDos campaign has now collected data from 191 participants
  across 9 sites in 7 countries — roughly 1.1 million minutes of
  eye-level and 1.7 million minutes of chest-level light exposure
  data, one of the largest multi-site light exposure efforts to date.
  Key discussion topics included data harmonisation as the field's
  biggest practical bottleneck, the need for consent-verification
  infrastructure, and the priority of building Global South research
  capacity.
