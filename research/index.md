      <h2>Circadian and Ocular Health</h2>

      <p>
        Our research bridges key knowledge gaps around light's impact
        on circadian rhythms and ocular health. We investigate how
        different light environments affect our biological clocks, sleep
        patterns, and overall well-being.
      </p>

      <p>
        By combining laboratory studies with real-world data collection,
        we're developing a more nuanced understanding of how light
        exposure throughout the day influences health outcomes
        across different populations and settings.
      </p>
