  <section class="about-section">
    <h2>Problem statement</h2>

    <p>
      Understanding how light and optical radiation affects human health requires
      high-quality, comparable data collected across diverse environments, devices,
      and populations. Yet the current landscape of light-exposure research is
      fragmented: there is no common approach to collecting or structuring data,
      measurement methods vary widely, metadata is inconsistent or incomplete,
      and datasets are often inaccessible. This lack of alignment extends to
      analysis tools and workflows, which are siloed and independently recreated
      across research groups, making cross-study comparisons, meta-analyses, and
      evidence synthesis difficult and preventing the development of sustained,
      cumulative research practices. Without shared standards or infrastructure,
      researchers, clinicians, and policymakers lack the foundation needed to
      interpret light-exposure data reliably and at scale.
    </p>
  </section>


  <section class="about-section about-section-soft">
    <h2>Our Vision</h2>

    <p>
      We envision a world where high-quality, real-world light-exposure and optical
      radiation data is standardised, interoperable, and globally inclusive,
      forming a trusted evidence base for understanding how light affects human
      physiology, behaviour, and health. The Global Light Commons aims to ensure
      that data collected globally across cultures, climates, and lived environments
      can be reliably compared and integrated, supporting equitable scientific
      discovery, improved health outcomes, and informed decision-making worldwide.
      This vision includes a collaborative global research environment – one where
      shared standards, open tools, and cross-cultural partnerships enable insights
      that no single group or dataset could achieve alone.
    </p>
  </section>


  <section class="about-section">
    <h2>Our Mission</h2>

    <p>
      Our mission is to establish and maintain the shared standards, infrastructure,
      and collaborative ecosystem needed to make light-exposure data a harmonised,
      openly accessible, and scientifically rigorous global resource. We work to:
    </p>

    <ul class="mission-list">
      <li>
        <strong>Define common data standards and metadata schemas</strong>
        that support interoperability across devices, research groups, and
        measurement environments.
      </li>

      <li>
        <strong>Develop open, validated tools and pipelines</strong>
        for collecting, processing, and analysing real-world light-exposure data,
        including data from wearable technologies.
      </li>

      <li>
        <strong>Harmonize archival and contemporary datasets</strong>
        within a durable, FAIR-aligned data commons that supports longitudinal,
        globally representative, and cross-cultural research.
      </li>

      <li>
        <strong>Promote global representation, equity, and cross-cultural collaboration</strong>,
        enabling contributions from diverse populations, settings, and research
        contexts.
      </li>

      <li>
        <strong>Facilitate reproducible and cumulative science</strong>
        through shared methods, transparent research practices, and active knowledge
        exchange across institutions.
      </li>

      <li>
        <strong>Cultivate an engaged research community</strong>
        through workshops, partnerships, and collaborative projects that advance
        methodological innovation and shared learning.
      </li>

      <li>
        <strong>Translate harmonised evidence into impact</strong>,
        facilitating innovative, interdisciplinary research and informing policy
        development.
      </li>
    </ul>

    <p>
      The <em>Global Light Commons</em> provides the foundation, platform and
      springboard for coordinated, global light-and-health research.
    </p>
  </section>


  <section class="about-section about-section-soft">
    <h2>Research Focus</h2>

    <p>
      The GLC database serves as an enduring resource for the scientific community.
      It preserves and enhances the usability of archival data alongside new
      research, enabling longitudinal studies and comparative analyses over time.
      By consolidating global research efforts, GLC not only accelerates scientific
      discovery but also provides crucial insights that inform public health
      policies and clinical applications and, ultimately, improve patient outcomes
      worldwide.
    </p>

    <p>
      Through its commitment to collaboration and community engagement, the GLC
      project aims to build a legacy of innovation and sustained progress in
      understanding and managing the health impacts of global light exposure.
    </p>
  </section>


  <section class="about-section">
    <h2>Global Collaboration</h2>

      (TUM GIF), enabling us to build international partnerships and conduct
      cross-cultural research. Our workshops bring together researchers from around
      the world to share knowledge and develop new approaches to understanding
      light's impact on health.
    </p>
