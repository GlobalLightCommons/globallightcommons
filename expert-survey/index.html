How private is light exposure data?

As we build open infrastructure for harmonizing real-world light exposure data, we want to better understand its ethical and privacy implications. In particular, we are interested in how this data fits within a two-dimensional privacy framework based on identifiability (how easily individuals can be identified) and health inferentiality (how much information about a person's health can be inferred from the data).

Your anonymous responses will directly inform the GLC's data governance and FAIR data-sharing frameworks.
