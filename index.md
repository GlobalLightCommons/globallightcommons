<section class="research-focus">
  <div class="research-focus-inner">
    <h2 class="research-focus-title">Our Research Focus</h2>

    <div class="research-focus-grid">
      <article class="research-focus-card">
        <h3>Light Exposure Data</h3>
        <p>
          We're reimagining how light exposure data is collected, stored,
          processed, and analyzed to build a more comprehensive understanding
          of the visual environment.
        </p>
      </article>

      <article class="research-focus-card">
        <h3>Circadian Health</h3>
        <p>
          Our research bridges key knowledge gaps around light's impact on
          circadian and ocular health, exploring how different light
          environments affect our biological rhythms.
        </p>
      </article>

      <article class="research-focus-card">
        <h3>Cross-Cultural Perspectives</h3>
        <p>
          We explore environmental, political and social differences researchers
          face, emphasizing the need for continued dialogue and information
          sharing across cultures.
        </p>
      </article>
    </div>
