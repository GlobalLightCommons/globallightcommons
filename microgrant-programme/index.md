      <h1>Micro-grant Programme</h1>

      <p class="microgrant-hero-lead">
        Supporting targeted activities that contribute data and expertise
        to the Global Light Commons.
      </p>

    </div>
  </section>


  <section class="microgrant-intro">
    <div class="microgrant-intro-copy">

      <p>
        The Global Light Commons is a new open infrastructure and sharing platform
        for light exposure and visual experience data, developed by the
        <a
          href="https://www.sg.tum.de/tscn"
          target="_blank"
          rel="noopener noreferrer"
        >
          Translational Sensory &amp; Circadian Neuroscience Unit
        </a>
        (Max Planck Society / Technical University of Munich / TUMCREATE).
      </p>

      <p>
        The overarching goal of the Global Light Commons is to bring together
        heterogeneous light-related datasets in a
        <a
          href="https://www.go-fair.org/fair-principles/"
          target="_blank"
          rel="noopener noreferrer"
        >
          FAIR
        </a>
        manner – Findable, Accessible, Interoperable, and Reusable – with a strong
        emphasis on harmonised data structures, metadata and metrics to enable
        cross-study and cross-region analyses.
      </p>

      <p>
        As part of the Global Light Commons, we are launching a micro-grant
        programme to support targeted activities that contribute data and
        expertise to the platform. Funding is offered under two complementary
        tracks:
      </p>

    </div>
  </section>


  <section class="grant-tracks">
    <div class="grant-tracks-inner">

      <article class="grant-card">

        <div class="grant-card-top">
          <span class="grant-track-label">Track 1</span>
          <span class="grant-amount">EUR 3,000</span>
        </div>

        <h2>
          Data collection &amp; harmonisation in LMICs
        </h2>

        <ul>
          <li>
            Capturing light exposure and/or visual experience data in
            under-represented regions
          </li>

          <li>
            Harmonising newly collected datasets to open, shared standards
          </li>

          <li>
            Enabling global reuse of data through structured metadata and
            documentation
          </li>
        </ul>

      </article>


      <article class="grant-card">

        <div class="grant-card-top">
          <span class="grant-track-label">Track 2</span>
          <span class="grant-amount">EUR 1,500</span>
        </div>

        <h2>
          Harmonisation of existing datasets
        </h2>

        <ul>
          <li>
            Mapping legacy or proprietary formats to Global Light Commons
            standards
          </li>

          <li>
            Improving metadata quality and interoperability
          </li>

          <li>
            Preparing datasets for open sharing and long-term reuse
          </li>
        </ul>

      </article>

    </div>
  </section>


  <section class="microgrant-section microgrant-section-soft">
    <div class="microgrant-section-heading">
      <span class="microgrant-section-number">01</span>
      <h2>What We Support</h2>
    </div>

    <div class="microgrant-section-body">

      <p>
        Micro-grants may be used for activities such as:
      </p>

      <ul>
        <li>Data curation and restructuring</li>
        <li>Metadata development and documentation</li>
        <li>Harmonisation to agreed-upon metrics and schemas</li>
        <li>Preparation of datasets for FAIR sharing</li>
      </ul>

      <p>
        The programme is explicitly designed to lower barriers to participation,
        recognise the value of data stewardship work, and accelerate the creation
        of a globally inclusive, interoperable resource for light exposure and
        visual experience research.
      </p>

    </div>
  </section>


  <section class="microgrant-section">
    <div class="microgrant-section-heading">
      <span class="microgrant-section-number">02</span>
      <h2>Eligibility Criteria</h2>
    </div>

    <div class="microgrant-section-body">

      <div class="criteria-block">
        <h3>Applicant profile</h3>

        <ul>
          <li>
            Researchers, data scientists, practitioners, or institutions working
            with light exposure and/or visual experience data
          </li>

          <li>
            Applicants may be based anywhere globally; LMIC-based applicants are
            particularly encouraged under Track 1
          </li>

          <li>
            Early-career researchers and teams with limited access to
            infrastructure are explicitly eligible
          </li>
        </ul>
      </div>


      <div class="criteria-block">
        <h3>Data relevance</h3>

        <ul>
          <li>
            Proposed activities must involve light exposure and/or visual
            experience data relevant to human environments (e.g. natural, built,
            occupational, clinical, or everyday settings)
          </li>

          <li>
            Datasets may be newly collected (Track 1) or pre-existing (Track 2)
          </li>
        </ul>
      </div>


      <div class="criteria-block">
        <h3>Commitment to open science</h3>

        <ul>
          <li>
            Applicants must commit to aligning datasets with the FAIR principles
          </li>

          <li>
            Resulting datasets must be prepared for open sharing via the Global
            Light Commons or a compatible open repository, subject to ethical and
            legal constraints
          </li>
        </ul>
      </div>


      <div class="criteria-block">
        <h3>Ethics and governance</h3>

        <ul>
          <li>
            Applicants are responsible for ensuring appropriate ethical approvals,
            consent, and compliance with local and international data protection
            regulations
          </li>

          <li>
            Data involving human participants must be shared in a de-identified
            or appropriately governed form
          </li>
        </ul>
      </div>

    </div>
  </section>


  <section class="microgrant-section microgrant-section-soft">
    <div class="microgrant-section-heading">
      <span class="microgrant-section-number">03</span>
      <h2>Evaluation Principles</h2>
    </div>

    <div class="microgrant-section-body">

      <p>
        Proposals will be evaluated according to the following principles, with
        an emphasis on data value and reusability rather than project scale:
      </p>


      <div class="evaluation-grid">

        <article class="evaluation-card">
          <h3>Contribution to the Global Light Commons</h3>

          <ul>
            <li>
              Degree to which the proposed work increases the coverage,
              diversity, or usability of the shared data resource
            </li>

            <li>
              Relevance to under-represented regions, populations, or environments
            </li>
          </ul>
        </article>


        <article class="evaluation-card">
          <h3>Data harmonisation &amp; FAIR alignment</h3>

          <ul>
            <li>
              Clarity and feasibility of the proposed harmonisation approach
            </li>

            <li>
              Quality and completeness of metadata, documentation, and standards
              alignment
            </li>

            <li>
              Expected interoperability with existing datasets in the Global
              Light Commons
            </li>
          </ul>
        </article>


        <article class="evaluation-card">
          <h3>Technical and methodological soundness</h3>

          <ul>
            <li>
              Appropriateness of data collection methods (Track 1) or curation
              strategies (Track 2)
            </li>

            <li>
              Realistic and well-scoped use of micro-grant funds
            </li>
          </ul>
        </article>


        <article class="evaluation-card">
          <h3>Open science &amp; reuse potential</h3>

          <ul>
            <li>
              Likelihood that the resulting dataset will be reusable by a broad
              research community
            </li>

            <li>
              Transparency of methods, assumptions, and limitations
            </li>
          </ul>
        </article>


        <article class="evaluation-card">
          <h3>Capacity building &amp; equity</h3>

          <ul>
            <li>
              Contribution to local capacity building, especially in LMIC contexts
            </li>

            <li>
              Evidence that the micro-grant meaningfully lowers barriers to
              participation in global data sharing efforts
            </li>
          </ul>
        </article>

      </div>

    </div>
  </section>
