  <section class="kickoff-exhibition">
    <h2>About the Exhibition</h2>

    <p>
      Luisa Baldinucci's "Afterglow" installation at Haus der Kunst explores the relationship between
      light, color, and human perception. The immersive environment creates a unique sensory experience
      that resonates with GLC's research into how light affects our health and well-being.
    </p>

    <p>
      The vibrant pink and blue lighting schemes create contrasting environments that demonstrate how
      dramatically different light conditions can alter our perception and potentially impact our
      physiological responses - a perfect complement to our research focus.
    </p>
